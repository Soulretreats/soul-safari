The Soul Safari Experience

Imagine...

01 Waking up to the sounds of the African bush
02 Meaningful mornings as you unroll your mat on the open air deck, looking out at the wild landscape of the Olifants River
03 Intimate animal encounters, guided Big 5 game drives and bush walks that will forever impact how you experience nature
04 Yoga and wellness practices to support modern life
05 Intuitive plant based local cuisine
06 Close out the evening around the fire, under the stars, recollecting the moments of another amazing day

This is the Soul Safari. The meeting point of wilderness and wellness.

Take some time away for yourself, dissolve those daily stresses and return to you.

A story in every moment

01
Movement

Yoga at the
river's edge

Mornings begin gently: mindful sips and munches, guided wilderness walks in the pre-dawn quiet. Meet Sandhiya on an open-air circular deck overlooking the river for a sunrise yoga practice blending movement, meditation and breathwork, as the bush wakes around you.

02
The Wild

Into the heart
of Kruger

Dusk and dawn game drives take you deep into the Greater Kruger - tracking the Big Five with expert field guides. Sundowners in the bush at golden hour and intimate wildlife experiences. There is no way to describe it.

03
Wonder

Bio-harmonise

Bio-harmonising is a holistic wellness practice that emphasizes aligning the body and mind to nature's rhythm. This helps achieve optimal physical, mental and emotional health and well-being by reconnecting to the natural world. The wild is not somewhere you go. It is simply where you are.

04
Nourishment

Food as
medicine

Three daily chef-prepared plant-based meals celebrate local flavours and seasonal abundance - beautifully plated, deeply nourishing. Eaten in community, in the open air, making every meal feel like a celebration.

Your Guide

Sandhiya

Sandhiya is a South African born yoga teacher and retreat leader, shaped by international experiences. She has guided wellness journeys for over 1,000 individuals, taught yoga to 1,200+ participants globally, and delivered 50+ retreats across India, Portugal, the UAE, and the UK. Her approach weaves practices that honour the traditions, whilst also being practical and relevant for our modern world.

The Soul Safari is her personal offering to you — Africa is home, and she brings that intimacy to each day of your retreat.
